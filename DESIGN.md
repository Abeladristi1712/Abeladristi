My notes:

I want a clean, architecture-led portfolio with large immersive project images, generous whitespace, and a calm editorial grid.

The navigation should feel simple and intuitive, with a clear separation between selected projects, sustainable design work, performance studies, and about/contact information.

I want to use warm off-white or light stone backgrounds, dark charcoal typography, and deep forest green accents. Images should feel natural, refined, and atmospheric, with greenery, daylight, materials, façade detail, and spatial experience as important visual elements.

## 8. Typography

- [ ] Sans-serif
- [ ] Serif
- [ ] Monospace
- [x] Serif + Sans
- [ ] Sans + Mono
- [ ] Other

Maximum: **2 typefaces**

What should the typography feel like?

Refined, calm, contemporary, and architectural. The typography should feel professional and human, balancing visual elegance with clarity for technical and sustainability-related information.

Use a distinctive but restrained serif typeface for large headings, project titles, and selected quotations. Use a clean sans-serif typeface for body text, navigation, captions, project metadata, and performance metrics.

The typography should have generous spacing, strong hierarchy, and comfortable reading sizes. It should never feel overly decorative, luxury-oriented, or difficult to read.

My distinctive move:

This creates a consistent signature across the portfolio: every project is presented as both an architectural experience and a measurable environmental-performance story.
